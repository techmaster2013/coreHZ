Mantle is a website that runs as an html file you download to your computer.
After downloading, you run the html file, and it gives you 4 options:
1. Mineral, a Minecraft launcher.
2. Slate, an AI.
3. Magma, a miniature OS.
4. The community discord server.

You can find Mantle at these links:
1. getmantle.pages.dev
2. getmantle.vercel.app
3. mantleunblocked.github.io
